Common Pixel problems

A battery that swells or lifts the screen needs replacing quickly. Green lines or a screen that flickers after a drop mean a new screen. If the phone only charges at a certain angle, the charging port is often just full of pocket fluff, and then we sort it out with a clean.

Questions about Pixel repair

Why do the Pixel prices say “from”?

Pixel parts are usually ordered in specially for your exact model. You get the exact price after the free check-up, before we do anything.

Will the fingerprint sensor and Face Unlock work after the repair?

Yes. The fingerprint sensor is calibrated and tested after a screen replacement, and we check the camera and face unlock before you get your phone back.

How long does it take?

The repair itself takes 30–60 minutes. If the part has to be ordered in, it typically takes 1–3 working days.
