Questions about Samsung repair

Why is a Samsung screen more expensive than an iPhone screen?

On the Galaxy S models, the screen is an AMOLED module with curved or thin glass and a built-in fingerprint reader. The part costs more, which is why the price is higher than on many iPhones.

Will the fingerprint reader and water resistance still work after the repair?

We set up the fingerprint reader again and test it before you get your phone back. We close the phone with a new seal, but no workshop can promise the same water resistance as from the factory.

Do you also repair the Galaxy Z Fold and Flip?

Yes, but the price depends a lot on the damage. Book a free check-up and you'll get a fixed price.
