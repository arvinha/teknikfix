Almost always. Choose “My model isn't on the list” when you book, and tell us the brand and model. We'll check the part and give you a fixed price before we start.

Nothing. Diagnosis is free, and if it can't be repaired, you pay 0 DKK.

No. For screen, battery and charging port repairs we don't touch your data. It's still a good idea to make a backup if your phone turns on.
