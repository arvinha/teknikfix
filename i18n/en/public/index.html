Questions

Do I have to pay before you repair it?

No. You see the price before you book, and you only pay when it works. If you'd rather pay straight away, you can do that too.

What if it can't be repaired?

Then it costs 0 DKK. The check-up is always free.

Can I just drop by?

No, only by appointment. Book a time and we'll confirm it by text message or e-mail. We can also come to you in Aalborg.

How long does it take?

A battery takes about 30 minutes and a screen about 45 minutes, when we have the part in stock. You'll see the time for your model when you book.

Do you also repair Google Pixel, OnePlus, Xiaomi and other brands?

Yes. We repair almost every brand of phone, computer and tablet. If your model isn't on the list, choose "My model isn't listed". Diagnostics are free, and you get a fixed price before we start.
