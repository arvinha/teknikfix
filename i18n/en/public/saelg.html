    Do you buy phones that are broken?

    Yes. A smashed screen, a worn-out battery, or one that won't turn on at all. We deduct what it costs us to repair it, and you see the price straight away.

    How do I get paid?

    By MobilePay or bank transfer, normally within 1 working day after we have checked the device. If you live outside Denmark, we send the money to your IBAN.

    Can I sell from another EU country?

    Yes. Choose your country and send the device with the shipping label we email you. The shipping cost of {{SELL_EU_SHIP}} DKK is deducted from the amount, and you get the money in your IBAN account.

    What if the device doesn't match the description?

    Then you get a new offer. If you say no, we send it back to you free of charge.

    What about my data?

    Make a backup, and feel free to reset the device. We erase everything before it is repaired and sold on.

    Why do I need to turn off Find My iPhone?

    A locked device is no use to anyone, so we can't buy it. Go to Settings → your name → Find My → Find My iPhone, and turn it off.
