Questions about prices

Is the price fixed?

Yes, when a price is shown without "from". That's the price you pay. If we find another fault, we'll tell you before we do anything, and the decision is yours.

What if it can't be repaired?

Then it costs 0 DKK. If you've paid in advance, you get all your money back.

How can I pay?

With {{PAY_LIST}}. You pay when the repair is finished and working.

Is there a guarantee?

Yes. If the same fault comes back within 30 days, we'll fix it again at no extra charge.
