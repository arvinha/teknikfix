Which Mac models?

MacBook Air and MacBook Pro with Intel and Apple chips (M1 to M4). We also help with iMac and Mac mini. Enter the model when you book, or bring it along and we'll find it together.

The battery in a MacBook

Under System Settings, Battery you can see whether your Mac says "Service Recommended". If it does, it's time for a new battery. If it only runs with the charging cable plugged in, the battery is almost always the cause.

Questions about MacBook repair

Can you rescue the files on a MacBook that won't start?

Often, yes. We'll take a look at it for free and tell you whether the files can be saved before you pay anything.

Why do screen and battery prices say "from"?

The part depends on the year and size of your MacBook. You get the fixed price after the free check-up, before we order the part.

Can an old MacBook be fast again?

Often, yes. A clean-up, update and tidy-up helps most of them. If it's an older model with a hard drive, an SSD makes it noticeably faster.
