Questions about computer repair

My computer has become slow. Can it be saved?

Usually, yes. It's often a clean-up, a program running in the background, or an old hard drive. An SSD can make an older computer noticeably faster.

Can you rescue my files?

If the drive can still be read, we copy your files over before we do anything else. If the drive is physically dead, we tell you straight away.

Do you come to my home and set up a new computer?

Yes. We move your files and set up email, printer and backup, in Aalborg with no call-out fee.
