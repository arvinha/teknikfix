Signs of a worn-out battery

Your phone switches off even though there's charge left. It gets hot when charging. It slows down because it's throttling to save the battery. Or the battery has started to swell, so the screen or back is lifting. If that's the case, it needs replacing straight away.

Which battery do we use?

New, high-quality batteries with full capacity. We test charging and capacity before you get your phone back.

Questions about battery replacement

When does the battery need replacing?

When your phone switches off at 20–30%, gets hot, or the battery health is below 80%. On an iPhone you can check this under Settings, Battery, Battery Health.

How long does a battery replacement take?

About 30 minutes for a phone, if the battery is in stock. 2–3 hours for an iPad, and typically 1–2 days for a laptop.

Will I lose my data?

No. A battery replacement doesn't touch your photos, messages or apps.
