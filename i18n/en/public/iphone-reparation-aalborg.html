Typical signs

The battery needs replacing when the phone shuts down at 20–30%, gets hot or shows less than 80% battery capacity. The charging port is often just full of pocket lint, and then cleaning it costs the lower end of the price.

Questions about mobile phone repair

How long does an iPhone battery replacement take?

Usually under an hour, when we have the battery in stock. You can often wait while it's done.

Will I lose my photos during the repair?

No, replacing a screen, battery or charging port doesn't touch your data. If the phone still turns on, it's a good idea to make a backup first.

Do you also repair Samsung and other Android phones?

Yes. Samsung, OnePlus, Google Pixel, Xiaomi and most others. Put the model in your booking and we'll check the part.
