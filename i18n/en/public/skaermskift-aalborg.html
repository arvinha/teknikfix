Questions about screen replacement

Can I keep using my phone with a cracked screen?

Often yes, but glass splinters can cut you and moisture gets in more easily. Put tape over the crack and get it replaced soon.

What's the difference between an original and a compatible screen?

An original screen has the same colours, brightness and touch as it had from the factory. A compatible screen is cheaper and works well for most people. You get the price for both and choose for yourself.

What if something else is wrong too?

Then we'll tell you before we go any further. You don't pay for anything you haven't agreed to.
